Scaled Dot-Product Attention — the formula

Attention is a three-step pipeline: similarity → normalize → weighted sum.
Attention(Q,K,V)  =  softmax( QK⊤√dk ) V
STEP 1
QK⊤
Dot product — how similar is every query to every key.
STEP 2
÷ √dk
Scale so variance stays stable for large dk — keeps gradients healthy.
STEP 3
softmax
Turn raw scores into probabilities that sum to 1.
STEP 4
· V
Weighted sum of values → the final context vector.
softmax: squashes any vector of numbers into probabilities that sum to 1.
gradient: the signal used to update weights — if it vanishes, learning stalls.
