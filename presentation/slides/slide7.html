Future positions get zero weight

Why mask?

Without mask, the model sees the answer it's supposed to predict — reducing generation to a trivial copy operation.

How it works

  • Create upper-triangular matrix filled with −∞
  • Add mask to attention scores before softmax
  • After softmax: −∞ → 0 weight
  • Lower triangle = allowed attention (past + present)

Bonus: The mask enables parallel training — all positions train simultaneously, unlike sequential RNN.

Takeaway: Causal mask = upper-triangular −∞ matrix → prevents future information leakage.
