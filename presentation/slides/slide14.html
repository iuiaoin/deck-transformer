Wiring it up with torch.nn.Transformer

PyTorch ships the whole architecture as one class. The tricky part is getting the masks right.
# instantiate the full encoder-decoder model model = nn.Transformer( d_model=512, nhead=8, num_encoder_layers=6, num_decoder_layers=6, dim_feedforward=2048, ) # forward pass — masks are the interesting part out = model( src, tgt, src_key_padding_mask=src_pad, # hide <pad> in encoder tgt_mask=causal_mask, # no peeking at future memory_key_padding_mask=src_pad, # hide <pad> in cross-attn ) # at inference: decoder is autoregressive for step in range(max_len): logits = model(src, tgt_so_far, ...) tgt_so_far = append(tgt_so_far, argmax(logits[-1]))
